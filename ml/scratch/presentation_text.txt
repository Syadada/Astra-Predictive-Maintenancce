  Shape: '04 · CLEANING & FEATURE PIPELINE'
  Shape: 'Domain Knowledge Highlights'
  Shape: 'Q'
  Shape: 'Why is Kurtosis important for Bearing Faults?'
  Shape: 'In the early stages of bearing degradation, the vibration signal is dominated by short-duration sharp impact impulses. RMS has not changed much, but Kurtosis spikes sharply (> 3) — providing early warning weeks before failure.'
  Shape: 'Q'
  Shape: 'Why are Cross-Parameter Features important?'
  Shape: 'Temperature rise can be caused by increasing production loads, not damage. With the temperature-to-current ratio (temp_per_load), we can distinguish natural temperature rises from those indicating cooling issues or bearing friction.'
