Bearing Harmonic Flux
MEDIUM RISK

Characterized by high-frequency oscillation in the 12-15kHz range. Usually indicates early-stage lubrication failure in conveyor drives.

Last Updated: Oct 12
Thermal Manifold Drift
CRITICAL

Sudden drop in casing temperature despite constant load. Potential thermocouple decalibration or fluid bypass in the cooling jacket.

Last Updated: Nov 05
Neural Inference Lag
SYSTEMIC

Processing latency exceeding 450ms. Often linked to high network packet loss or edge-node buffer saturation during peak cycles.
