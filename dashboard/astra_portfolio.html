Unplanned motor breakdowns in Astra industrial manufacturing lines cause severe financial losses, production halts, and safety hazards. To solve this, MaintenX was built as an enterprise-grade AI Predictive Maintenance platform for induction motors and conveyor shafts. Our technical solution leverages 1-Hz telemetry ingestion into PostgreSQL, multi-domain feature extraction (stator current MCSA and vibration FFT), a 3-tier cascade machine learning pipeline, and a rule-based False Alarm Suppression Decision Engine. The platform provides explainable AI (XAI) diagnostics, role-based access security, and automated CMMS work order generation, successfully transforming reactive plant maintenance into precision predictive operations.

Industrial equipment telemetry across Astra factories originates from heterogeneous sensors. To establish a standardized benchmark, we harmonized 4 global datasets into a unified PostgreSQL schema (`raw_sensor_data`): 1) Paderborn Dataset (1.5 kW Induction Motor MCSA stator current at 64kHz), 2) CWRU Dataset (2 HP Bearing vibration FFT spectrum), 3) NASA IMS Dataset (35-day run-to-failure heavy conveyor shaft degradation), and 4) NASA CMAPSS Dataset (21-sensor turbofan RUL telemetry). Data is cleaned via 30-second automated jobs handling forward-fill imputation, outlier clipping, and RMS signal extraction before feature store ingestion.

Raw AI inference can introduce false positives due to transient electrical noise or temporary load changes. To prevent alert fatigue among maintenance staff, we engineered a Decision Engine combining: 1) Business Rule bounds (Current > 15A, Temp > 140°C, Vibration > 8mm/s), 2) 3-step persistence checks, 3) Multi-model consensus validation (Model A1, A2, and B must agree), and 4) Alert cooldown mechanisms. This architecture successfully achieved a 94.2% False Alarm Suppression rate, lowered system False Positive Rate below 1.2%, and operates with an end-to-end latency of just 12.5 ms.
