Methodology Comparison

LSTM Autoencoder vs. Isolation Forest expand_more

Isolation Forest excels on static data but cannot capture temporal dependencies. Our LSTM Autoencoder leverages long-term memory to detect anomalies that only manifest across specific time sequences.

TFT vs. Vanilla Transformer expand_more

The Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) is purpose-built for multi-horizon forecasting with variable selection networks, enabling the model to ignore irrelevant inputs at each time step — unlike standard Transformers.

SHAP vs. LIME expand_more

LIME is locally scoped and can be globally inconsistent. SHAP is grounded in Game Theory (Shapley Values), guaranteeing a fair and mathematically consistent distribution of feature contributions across all predictions.
